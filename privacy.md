# Bot Tester – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 October 2026**

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Bot Tester processes information when used within Discord servers.

## 1. Information We Process

To provide its music-related features, Bot Tester may temporarily process:

- **Discord User IDs:** To identify users interacting with bot commands.
- **Discord Server IDs:** To identify the server where the bot is being used.
- **Channel and Voice Channel IDs:** To provide playback in the appropriate channels.
- **Command Information:** To understand and respond to music commands.
- **Music Information:** Such as search requests, track titles and temporary playback queues.
- **Technical Information:** Information needed to diagnose errors and maintain the bot.

We do not intentionally collect Discord passwords, payment card details or account login credentials.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only as necessary to:

- Respond to music commands.
- Manage temporary music queues.
- Control playback sessions.
- Identify the correct server and channels.
- Maintain the bot's functionality.
- Troubleshoot technical issues.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

Bot Tester is designed to use temporary playback information rather than maintain a permanent database of user activity.

Playback information is intended to be kept only while necessary for the relevant music session or bot functionality.

We do not intentionally maintain permanent user profiles or long-term playback histories.

Technical information may also be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers as part of operating the bot. Their own retention and security practices may apply.

## 4. YouTube and Third-Party Services

Bot Tester uses YouTube as a music source.

When users request music, relevant search or playback information may be processed by YouTube or other services involved in providing the requested functionality.

These third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

YouTube Terms of Service:
https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

If Bot Tester uses YouTube API Services, additional YouTube API privacy and data-handling requirements apply.

## 5. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by:
- Discord, as part of the Discord platform.
- Hosting providers responsible for running Bot Tester.
- YouTube and relevant third-party music services.
- Technical service providers where necessary to operate the bot.

Information is not intentionally shared for unrelated advertising purposes.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by Bot Tester.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of personal information.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.

For privacy requests, contact us using the email address below.

## 8. Data Deletion Requests

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with your use of Bot Tester, contact us with your Discord User ID and details of your request.

We will review and respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Because Bot Tester is designed around temporary playback information, some information may already have been discarded when your request is received.
